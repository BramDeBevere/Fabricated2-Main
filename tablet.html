Full screen on iPhone

Safari on Apple devices can't be forced into full screen. To remove the browser bar and see the whole screen:

  1. Tap Share — the square with an up-arrow at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then confirm.
  3. Open the new Fabricated icon from your home screen. It runs full screen, with no browser bar.
