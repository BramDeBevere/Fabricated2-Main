# Fabricated 2.0

**Fabricated 2.0** is an interactive installation about Venezuela. It challenges how algorithms shape what people see online and shows a different side of the country—its history, nature, culture, and politics.

---

## Concept

Online algorithms often show the same kind of stories about a country. Fabricated 2.0 gives people another way to explore Venezuela by making choices with their bodies instead of clicking buttons.

Each session is different because the outcome depends on the group’s choices.

---

## How It Works

1. The experience starts with chaotic visuals and sound.  
   For the first 20 seconds, participants cannot control anything.

2. After that, two options appear on the screen.

3. Participants vote by moving their bodies. A camera tracks where they stand.

4. Each choice leads to two new options that go deeper into the topic.

5. In the end, participants reach a specific page about Venezuela. This is the reward page.

---

## The Four Themes

All choices are based on four main themes:

- History
- Nature
- Culture
- Politics

These themes lead to more detailed topics as the session continues.

---

## Participation

- Made for three participants at a time
- Anyone can join
- Every group gets a different result
- Once set up, the project can run by itself

---

## Goal

Fabricated 2.0 shows that there is more to Venezuela than what algorithms usually show online.
